Privacy Policy

Chymanhaya is a Kurdish food and calorie tracking app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information Stored on Your Device

Meal logs, favorites, pantry selections, health-condition choices, reminder settings, dark mode preferences, custom foods, and similar app data are stored locally on your device using SQLite.

Feedback You Send

If you use the feedback screen, the app sends your message, optional email address, app version, platform, and submission time to the app's Firebase database so the developer can review and respond to feedback.

App Version Checks

The app may contact Firebase to check whether a newer version is available. This helps show update prompts when needed.

Permissions

On Android, the app is configured to use internet access for feedback and update checks and vibration for haptic feedback. It does not require external storage access.

Health and Nutrition Data

Health-condition selections are used inside the app to filter food suggestions. The app is for general information and organization only and does not provide medical advice.

Children

Chymanhaya is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the GitHub repository or use the contact details provided in the app store listing.
